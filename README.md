# 🧭 Baosala Tangier

## 🌍 Discover Tangier Differently

Baosala Tangier is a tourism web application designed to help visitors discover Tangier through a more local, authentic, and personalized experience.

## 📌 Summary

Tangier is a city rich in history, culture, food, traditional crafts, and unique local experiences. However, visitors often discover the city through the same well-known tourist destinations and may miss many authentic experiences.

**Baosala Tangier** aims to solve this problem by bringing different aspects of the Tangier experience together in one digital platform.

The application allows travelers to:

- 📍 Discover places and cultural experiences
- 🍲 Explore Moroccan food
- 🎨 Discover artisan workshops
- 🏨 Explore stays
- 🎯 Find local activities
- 🗺️ Create a personalized travel plan
- 👥 Discover other travelers
- 💬 Read notes from previous travelers
- 🗣️ Learn useful Darija expressions
- 💱 Convert currencies
- 🌍 Use the application in multiple languages

---

## 📌 Project Details

### What exactly does it do?

**Baosala Tangier** is a tourism platform focused on connecting travelers with the local identity of Tangier.

Instead of acting as a simple tourist guide, the platform combines discovery, planning, local culture, and traveler interaction.

The user can:

- Explore different places in Tangier
- Discover Moroccan food
- Find artisan workshops
- Explore activities and stays
- Add experiences to their travel plan
- Discover other traveler profiles
- Read experiences shared by previous travelers
- Use useful travel tools

---

## 🔴 The Problem

### The disconnection between mass tourism and authentic cultural experiences

Despite Tangier's important historical and cultural identity, visitors can have difficulty discovering experiences that are outside the traditional tourist path.

Some of the main problems are:

- Information is scattered across different platforms.
- Local artisan workshops can be difficult to discover.
- Visitors may miss authentic Moroccan food experiences.
- Travelers often depend on generic tourist recommendations.
- Planning different experiences requires several platforms.
- Visitors may have limited opportunities to connect with other travelers.

This can create a gap between **tourism** and the **local cultural experience** of Tangier.
